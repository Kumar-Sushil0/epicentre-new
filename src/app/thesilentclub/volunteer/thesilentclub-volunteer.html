Volunteer

Work here.
Live here.
Think here.

Minimum 3 months

This is not an internship. There is no syllabus, no performance review, no certificate at the end. You contribute to what makes this place possible — and in return, you get to live inside it. That exchange is the whole arrangement.

What you receive

The exchange

Food and accommodation for the full duration of your volunteer period
Full access to the estate — all spaces, all activities, all meals
An immersive experience inside a practice of silence and intentional living
Time. Unstructured, protected time to think, create, and be
What you contribute

Volunteer roles

Facility Management
Keeping the estate running quietly and without friction.
Vendor Communication
Coordinating with external suppliers and service providers.
Guest Hospitality
Ensuring members arrive and settle in without needing to ask for anything.
Content & Documentation
Capturing what happens here — in words, photographs, or film.
